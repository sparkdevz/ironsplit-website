🏋️ 4-Day Upper/Lower Split
Day 1 — Upper Body (Push Focus)
Monday · Chest · Shoulders · Triceps
ChestUpper ChestShouldersTriceps
💡 Rest 2–3 min between compound sets, 60–90 sec for isolation. Log weights every session and aim to beat last week.
Day 2 — Lower Body (Quad Focus)
Tuesday · Quads · Glutes · Hamstrings · Calves · Core
QuadsGlutesHamstringsCalvesCore
💡 Squat first when fresh. Core braced on every lower body movement. Drive through mid-foot.
🔄
Wednesday — Active Recovery
Light walk, stretching, or mobility work. Keep heart rate below 120 bpm. Your muscles are repairing right now.
Day 3 — Upper Body (Pull Focus)
Thursday · Back · Biceps · Rear Delts
LatsMid-BackTrapsBicepsRear Delts
💡 Drive elbows to hips, not your hands. Squeeze the back at peak contraction on every rep.
Day 4 — Lower Body (Hinge/Glute Focus)
Friday · Hamstrings · Glutes · Adductors · Calves · Core
HamstringsGlutesAdductorsCalvesCore
💡 Hip thrust: squeeze hard at the top for 1–2 sec every rep. Log your weights — this day responds fast to progressive overload.
😴
Saturday — Full Rest
Prioritise 7–9 hours of sleep. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep — this is when you actually build muscle.
🥗
Sunday — Full Rest
Hydrate, hit your protein target (1.6–2.2 g/kg bodyweight), and prep meals for the week ahead. Recovery is half the work.
