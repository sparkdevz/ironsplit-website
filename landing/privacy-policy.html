Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

IronSplit ("the App") is a workout tracking application developed for personal fitness use. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

IronSplit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account or internet connection to function.

Data Storage

All workout data — including logged sets, reps, weights, exercise swaps, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only using the device's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by the developer or any third party.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect certain device information and identifiers to serve ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's settings.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob for advertising. No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out through the Google Play Store listing page.
