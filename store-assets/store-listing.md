## Full Description

**IronSplit** is a clean, focused workout tracker built for the classic 4-day Upper/Lower training split — one of the most effective programs for building strength and muscle.

**4 Training Days**
- 🏋️ Monday — Upper Body Push (Chest, Shoulders)
- 🦵 Tuesday — Lower Body Quad Focus (Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings)
- 💪 Thursday — Upper Body Pull (Lats, Mid-Back, Traps)
- 🔥 Friday — Lower Body Hinge Focus (Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back)

**Key Features**

📊 **Set & Rep Logging** — Log weight and reps for every set. Inputs turn green as you complete them so you always know where you are in your session.

📈 **Week-over-Week Comparison** — See last week's numbers right next to your current sets with ▲▼ arrows showing if you're progressing.

🔄 **Exercise Variations** — Not feeling a particular exercise? Swap it for one of 3 curated alternatives per exercise without losing your tracking data.

🗓️ **12-Week Program** — Structured as a full 12-week progressive block. Switch between weeks using the week selector and review past sessions any time.

⚖️ **kg / lbs Toggle** — Switch between kilograms and pounds instantly. Your preference is saved automatically.

🌙 **Dark & Light Mode** — Easy on the eyes day or night. Theme preference is remembered between sessions.

📷 **Exercise Demonstrations** — Each exercise includes a demonstration image and coaching notes so you know exactly how to perform it correctly.

No account required. No internet needed during workouts. All your data stays on your device.
